Become a Volunteer

Give your time, skills and energy to help create positive change in our community.

Why Volunteer With Us?

Volunteers play an important role in helping Rise Together Foundation deliver community programmes and support individuals and families.

By volunteering, you can contribute your skills, meet new people and make a meaningful difference in the lives of others.

Volunteer Opportunities

Food Distribution

Help organise and distribute food parcels to community members.

Community Events

Assist with planning and running community events and outreach activities.

Education Support

Help learners with educational activities, resources and community projects.

Donation Collection

Assist with organising donated food, clothing and other essential items.

Volunteer Requirements

  • Be willing to work as part of a team.
  • Be respectful and considerate towards others.
  • Be reliable and committed to agreed activities.
  • Have a positive attitude.
  • Follow the organisation's guidelines.

Benefits of Volunteering

Make a Difference

Help improve the lives of people within your community.

Develop Skills

Gain valuable teamwork, communication and organisational experience.

Meet People

Connect with other volunteers and members of the community.
